Many sites ask for a refresher about every two years

The Statement of Attainment has no formal expiry date. Two-year refresher cycles are generally driven by site rules, employer requirements and industry guidance.

Working at Heights
Confined Space and gas test

We can say

Many employers and worksites expect refresher training about every two years. Check the requirements of the specific employer or site.

We should avoid

Do not claim either qualification automatically expires after two years, or that a standard mandates this interval universally.

CourseWhat the sources say
Confined SpaceThe national Model Code of Practice says refresher training should be provided as appropriate to the workplace, with frequency depending on how often workers undertake confined-space tasks. The National Training Register entry for an older unit says AS 2865 calls for reassessment at appropriate intervals and describes a two-to-three-year cycle as industry good practice. Neither source establishes a universal two-year expiry. Training records must be kept for two years; that is not a refresher interval.
Working at HeightsAS 2865 is a confined-spaces standard, not the basis for Working at Heights refreshers. There is no universal two-year expiry established by the national guidance reviewed. The Victorian Department of Education specifically requires school staff and volunteers who work at heights to refresh every two years. Other employers and worksites may set their own requirements.

Scripts for calls, texts and replies

From the strategy draft's booking questions:

2. Do they work at heights or in confined spaces?
3. When did you last renew?

WorkSafe Victoria-approved providers

WorkSafe maintains an official provider directory. Initial HSR OHS training is five days; the refresher is one day. Prices and dates are provider-specific and should be checked directly.

Kallibr opportunity: confirm current approval status and commercial case before proposing an initial HSR offer; refresher training is an annual entitlement.
